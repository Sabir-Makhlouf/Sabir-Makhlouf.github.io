1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Tech Science Lab ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile applications, games, and software ("Apps"). We build privacy-focused, offline-capable apps and do not require registration or personal information such as your name, home address, or phone number for core functionality.

2. Local Offline Storage

Core progress, scores, streaks, statistics, settings (such as language, theme, or audio/haptics), and game saves are stored strictly locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to developer-owned servers. Uninstalling the App or clearing App storage from your device settings deletes all locally stored data.

3. Voluntary Contact Information

If you contact us by email for support, feedback, or privacy inquiries, we receive your email address and message content only for the purpose of responding to and resolving your request.

4. Advertising, Analytics & Third-Party Services

When your device has an active internet connection, our Apps and Games may use trusted third-party SDKs such as Google AdMob, the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent SDK, and Google Play Services / Google Play Billing. These providers may automatically collect and process limited data including:

  • Device advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / AAID)
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location inferred from IP
  • Device model, OS version, app version, and language settings
  • App interactions, ad impressions/clicks, and diagnostic/crash logs
  • Purchase verification data when making an optional in-app purchase via Google Play

Review Google's privacy documentation for further details:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Partner Apps
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Compliance

5. GDPR, UK & U.S. State Privacy Consent Rights

In regions covered by privacy regulations (including the EU/UK GDPR and applicable U.S. state laws such as CCPA/CPRA), our Apps present a consent prompt powered by Google UMP before serving personalized ads. You may review or update your consent preferences inside the App's Settings → Manage Ad Privacy Choices (where available) or adjust/reset your Android Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Permissions

Our Apps request only permissions essential for their declared functionality (such as network access and advertising ID compliance). We do not access sensitive hardware or personal files unless explicitly disclosed and granted by you inside an App feature.

7. Data Security & Retention

Network requests made by integrated third-party SDKs use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Local app and game data remains on your device only until cleared or uninstalled.

8. Children's Privacy

Unless explicitly designated for children on its store page, our Apps and Games are designed for general audiences and are not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data, please email us so we can ensure prompt removal.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy occasionally as our Apps or legal requirements evolve. Updates take effect when published on this page with a revised Effective Date.
