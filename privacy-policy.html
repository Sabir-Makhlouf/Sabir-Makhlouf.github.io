1. Information Collection and Use

Our applications do not require account registration, and we do not directly collect or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, physical address, or phone number) on our own servers. All core application data, user calculations, preferences, and local files remain stored locally on your device.

However, our applications integrate trusted third-party services that may collect technical identifiers used to identify your device for advertising, analytics, and crash diagnostics:

  • Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID): Used to serve relevant advertisements and measure ad performance.
  • Device & Technical Data: Device model, operating system version, IP address (for approximate region and privacy consent compliance), language settings, and crash logs.
  • App Interactions: Anonymous interaction events (such as ad impressions, clicks, and app launch diagnostics) used to maintain stability and prevent fraud.

2. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Sharing

We share necessary technical identifiers solely with trusted third-party SDK providers integrated into our covered applications to operate core features and display advertisements:

  • Google Play Services: Provides core Android runtime and security functionality. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob: Used to display banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open advertisements. AdMob may collect and process your Advertising ID, IP address, and usage data. (How Google uses information from sites or apps)
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used to request and manage user privacy consent choices in compliance with GDPR and CCPA regulations.

3. User Rights, Consent & Opt-Out Choices

You have full control over your privacy and advertising preferences:

  • Consent Management: Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and applicable regions can review or withdraw their ad personalization consent at any time via the in-app privacy settings or consent dialog.
  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: You can opt out of personalized advertising or reset/delete your Advertising ID anytime through your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.
  • Local Data Deletion: Since your app data is stored locally on your device, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the application permanently deletes all local app data.

4. Data Security

All network communications with third-party advertising services are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

Our covered applications are intended for a general audience and are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through third-party services, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action.
